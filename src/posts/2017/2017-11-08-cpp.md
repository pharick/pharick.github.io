I worked through a two-year C++ exercise course, organized into numbered lessons
from first programs, conditionals and loops up to pointers, dynamic and 2D
arrays, file I/O and a small Tetris. The second year covers recursion, strings,
classes, constructors and copy semantics, operator overloading, templates,
inheritance, virtual functions and the STL (string, vector, algorithm).
