I built CamlCast, a first-person raycasting engine in OCaml on SDL2 (tsdl),
released under MIT with CI, release bundles for macOS, Linux and Windows, and
published documentation. Worlds are graphs of rooms with walls at any angle,
sloped floors, ceilings or open sky, see-through walls, decals, sprites, doors
and portals, with the floor, ceiling and sky cast per pixel by a software
renderer. Games describe their world declaratively every frame through a small
React-like runtime (`loom`) with components, hooks and reconciliation built on
OCaml 5 effect handlers; twenty-two demos, a step-by-step game-building guide,
and Alcotest suites that render frames offscreen accompany the engine.
