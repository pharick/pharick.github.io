I built ft_transcendence, a full-stack multiplayer web application with a
NestJS/TypeORM/PostgreSQL backend and a Next.js/React frontend, deployed with
docker-compose. The backend has École 42 OAuth and JWT login with two-factor
auth, real-time chat rooms and direct messages, friends and invites,
matchmaking, live games with spectators and completed-game history, all over
Socket.IO gateways.

**Language:** TypeScript
