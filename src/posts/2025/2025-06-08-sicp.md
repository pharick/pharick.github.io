I worked through exercises from Structure and Interpretation of Computer
Programs in Racket, covering chapters 1 to 3: recursive and iterative processes,
fast exponentiation, primality tests, higher-order procedures and fixed points,
rational and interval arithmetic, list and tree operations, folds and
accumulations. Each exercise is a separate .rkt file named after its number.
