I built libftpp, a modern C++23 static library of reusable components: an RAII
object pool and an endianness-aware DataBuffer,
Singleton/Observer/Memento/StateMachine patterns, threading utilities (named
Thread, ThreadSafeQueue, WorkerPool, PersistentWorker, thread-safe iostream),
non-blocking TCP Client/Server with typed messages, and math types (2D/3D
vectors, Perlin noise, a deterministic coordinate hash). Everything is covered
by Catch2 tests and per-component example programs.
