I made CatKiller, a small 2D game in Godot 4.2 written in GDScript. It has a
pixel-art cat knight character with idle and run animations, a gun that fires
bullets, slime enemies, obstacles and explosion effects, all organized as Godot
scenes and scripts.

**Language:** GDScript  
**Started:** 2024-08
