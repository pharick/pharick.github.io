I built scop, a small OpenGL 3D model viewer in C++ using GLFW and GLEW. It
parses Wavefront .obj files and BMP textures with my own loaders, has hand-
written matrix and quaternion math, and renders models with color and texture
GLSL shaders; sample models such as the 42 logo, a teapot and a monkey are
included.

**Language:** C++
