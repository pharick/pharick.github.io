I built cub3D, a raycasting-based level rendering engine in C, as an École 42
project. It loads levels from a text file, renders walls with a different
texture on each of the four sides plus 2D sprites, and lets you walk with WASD
and look around with the arrow keys. It targets macOS only.

**Language:** C
