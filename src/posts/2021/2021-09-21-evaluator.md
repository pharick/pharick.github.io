I wrote a C++ program that evaluates integer arithmetic expressions with +, -,
*, / and parentheses while respecting operator precedence. It converts the
expression to Reverse Polish Notation before evaluating it and prints the step-
by-step RPN evaluation; fractional results are handled correctly even though
operands must be integers.
