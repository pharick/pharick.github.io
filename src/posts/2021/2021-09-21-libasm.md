I wrote a static library in x86 assembly (NASM, macOS) as an École 42 project.
It implements strlen, strcpy, strcmp, write and read (via syscalls), strdup
(using malloc), strchr and atoi_base, plus singly-linked list helpers:
list_push_front, list_size, list_sort and list_remove_if.

**Language:** C  
**Started:** 2021-09 · **Last activity:** 2023-06
