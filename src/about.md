# About me

I'm **Artem Forkunov**, a software engineer with a taste for low-level things:
C and C++, systems programming, graphics, and lately functional programming in OCaml.

This site is my public log of how I feed my brain. Every entry on the
[timeline](/) is something I built, finished, read or finally understood.
It doubles as a living CV: instead of a static list of skills, you can scroll
back through the years and see what I was actually doing.

## What you'll find here

- **Projects** — repositories I built, most of them open on [GitHub](https://github.com/pharick).
- **Courses** — things I studied and completed.
- **Books** — what I read and what stuck.
- **Concepts** — ideas that took a while to click.
- **Milestones** — the bigger checkpoints along the way.
